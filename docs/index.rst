********************************************************************************
compas_vol
********************************************************************************

Volumetric modelling with signed distance functions, for **COMPAS 2**.

A fork of `dbt-ethz/compas_vol <https://github.com/dbt-ethz/compas_vol>`_ by
Mathias Bernhard (Digital Building Technologies, ETH Zurich), ported to COMPAS 2
and NumPy 2 and extended with processes, fabrication checks, assembly and
Grasshopper components. :doc:`porting` lists the differences.
