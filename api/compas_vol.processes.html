compas_vol.processes

Geometry made by process rather than by construction: erosion, growth, phase separation. Where the rest of the package describes a shape with an expression, these iterate on a sampled field and let the result emerge.

All of them share one operator. A process moves the surface along its normal at a speed that varies across it:

phi <- phi + speed * |grad phi|      then redistance

A positive speed takes material away, a negative one adds it. Weathering and growth are the same loop with the sign flipped, which is why they are not separate machinery, and why what distinguishes one process from another is its speed term, not its code.

Three rules hold the subpackage together:

  1. Everything returns a valid distance field. Redistancing is part of the loop, not the caller’s problem, because every operation downstream – shells, offsets, thicknesses in millimetres – depends on it.

  2. Everything is in model units. Including the lengths that curvature and shelter are measured against. A curvature term compared against the voxel size instead of a real length is the difference between a coral and a ball.

  3. :class:`compas_vol.primitives.VolGrid` is the way back. Processes are grid-native; wrapping the result returns it to the modelling world, where it can be shelled, cut, filled with a lattice and exported.

What a process may not touch is said once, on the field: Field.constrain() keeps a solid (a beam, a deck) and an envelope, and because every process ends its steps by redistancing, which is where the constraint is imposed, every process honours it.

Requires the processes extra (pip install compas-vol[processes]) for scikit-fmm, which does the redistancing.

Classes

Field

A distance field on a regular grid, with the grid it lives on.

Functions

evolve

Move the surface along its normal, repeatedly.

redistance

Rebuild a signed distance field from its own zero level set.

lattice

Grow bounds outward onto a world lattice of spacing voxel.

opened

Remove everything thinner than 2 * radius, keeping the rest as it was.

closed

Fill every gap narrower than 2 * radius, keeping the rest as it was.

value_noise

Smooth pseudo-random noise in [-1, 1], from world coordinates.

fbm

Fractal noise: several octaves of value_noise(), summed.

hardness

A noise field standing in for how resistant the rock is, in [-1, 1].

abrasion

Rounding: what sticks out wears first.

differential

Soft rock retreats, hard rock stands proud.

pitting

Hollows deepen faster than the faces around them: tafoni, honeycombing.

strata

Bedding: alternating hard and soft layers, weathering unevenly.

runoff

Rain: water gathers downhill and cuts where it concentrates.

jointing

Joints: the mass breaks into blocks before it wears.

fingering

Growth that folds: the surface buckles into lobes and valleys.

accretion

Growth that fills hollows: material settles where it is sheltered.

diffusion_limited

Growth fed by a diffusing supply: the mechanism behind dendrites.

branching

Branching built as a graph, then drawn into the field.

spinodal

Phase separation: a bicontinuous foam, by Cahn-Hilliard.

reaction_diffusion

Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion, thresholded into a solid.
