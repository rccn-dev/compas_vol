Assembly

class compas_vol.assembly.Assembly(pieces, shape, bounds, clearance=0.0, interfaces=None)[source]

Bases: object

A model cut into pieces, and the record of how they go back together.

Iterate it to get Piece objects. Nothing is sampled until you ask for it, so building the assembly of a whole pavilion costs almost nothing – it is the pieces that cost, and only the ones you build.

pieces
Type:

list[Piece]

shape

How many pieces along each axis, for a grid; None for pieces cut from a list of cells.

Type:

tuple[int, int, int] | None

bounds

The whole model’s bounds.

Type:

tuple[float, …]

clearance

The gap left between neighbours, in model units.

Type:

float

interfaces

Every face two pieces share: low and high (the pieces below and above it), axis, position along that axis, and rect, the shared rectangle as (lo_a, lo_b, hi_a, hi_b) over the other two axes in order.

Type:

list[dict]

Methods

build

Build pieces one at a time: process each with its halo, then finish it.

check

Inspect every piece, and say which ones will not build.

cost

What it would take to build every piece at this resolution.

find

The piece with this name.

manifest

A bill of pieces, as text.

neighbours

The pieces sharing a face with this one.
